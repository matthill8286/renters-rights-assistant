# Possession check

1. Find the reason. Map it to a ground: moving in (1), selling (1A), serious arrears (8), anti-social behaviour (14). If it fits none of these, say the prototype does not cover it and suggest a solicitor.
2. Call `check_possession` with the tenancy id, the ground and the planned service date.
3. Answer in this order:
   - Can notice be served now? List any blockers first.
   - When the notice period ends, and the earliest date possession could take effect (the 12-month rule can push this later).
   - Warnings, such as deposit protection or arrears needing to stay at the threshold.
4. Note that Section 21 no longer exists, and that the notice uses Form 3A.
5. End with: a solicitor should review the notice before it is served.
